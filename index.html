Grammar I: Present Progressive (First Person Plural - We're)

We use the present progressive to talk about actions happening right now. For the first person plural ("we"), we use We're (which means We are) plus an action verb ending in -ing.

Question Answer Notes
What are you doing? We're reading. / cutting. / erasing. yellow paper. / the board. we're = we are

Grammar II: Questions with "Are there...?" & Short Answers

We use Are there any...? to ask about plural items in a classroom or place. We answer with short affirmative or negative forms.

Question Answer Notes
Are there any crayons / books? Yes, there are.
No, there aren't.		 there are / there aren't aren't = are not

Solved Examples (Levels: Basic, Intermediate, Advanced)

Review these fully solved examples based on Our World 2 Unit 1 guidelines.

BASIC - Example 1

Question: Look at the two students with scissors. What are they doing? Write the sentence.

Solution: We're cutting yellow paper.

Explanation: We use "We're" + verb ending in "-ing" (cutting) + object.

BASIC - Example 2

Question: Look at the classroom shelf. Are there any crayons?

Solution: Yes, there are.

Explanation: The shelf has a box labeled DRAWING with crayons, so we answer affirmatively.

BASIC - Example 3

Question: Complete with the correct action: "We're ________ the board." (erasing / reading)

Solution: We're erasing the board.

Explanation: We clean the board by erasing it.

BASIC - Example 4

Question: Look at the shelf section "WRITING". Are there any notebooks?

Solution: Yes, there are.

Explanation: Notebooks are clearly visible on the top shelf.

BASIC - Example 5

Question: Form the short negative answer for: Are there any paintbrushes? (assuming none are present)

Solution: No, there aren't.

Explanation: "aren't" is the contraction for "are not".

INTERMEDIATE - Example 6

Question: Look at the family in the kitchen cooking. What are they doing?

Solution: We're cooking.

Explanation: Multiple people in the kitchen cooking together use the first person plural pronoun group response.

INTERMEDIATE - Example 7

Question: Look at the family sitting on the sofa. Form the sentence: "What are you doing? We're ________ TV."

Solution: We're watching TV.

Explanation: The verb phrase for viewing a screen is "watching TV".

INTERMEDIATE - Example 8

Question: Look at the bottom shelf section "CUTTING". Are there any scissors?

Solution: Yes, there are.

Explanation: Scissors and hole punchers are in the cutting compartment.

INTERMEDIATE - Example 9

Question: Complete the question about classroom objects: "Are there ____ markers?"

Solution: Are there any markers?

Explanation: We use "any" in plural questions for items in a location.

INTERMEDIATE - Example 10

Question: Look at children eating snacks around a table. What are they doing?

Solution: We're eating.

Explanation: The action of consuming food is represented by "eating".

ADVANCED - Example 11

Question: Combine Grammar I and II: Look at two students counting coins into a jar and asking if there are any coins left. Form both the action statement and the question.

Solution: Action: "We're counting." Question: "Are there any coins?"

Explanation: Integrates present progressive action with plural existence questioning.

ADVANCED - Example 12

Question: Correct the grammatical error in this dialogue: "What are you doing? We are read a book."

Solution: "We're reading a book." (or "We are reading a book.")

Explanation: The verb must take the "-ing" suffix (reading) and contraction "We're" is preferred.

ADVANCED - Example 13

Question: Formulate a negative short answer and justify it based on a classroom shelf lacking paintbrushes: "Are there any paintbrushes?"

Solution: "No, there aren't. Paintbrushes are not on the shelf."

Explanation: Combines short negative answer with full contextual explanation.

ADVANCED - Example 14

Question: Create a dialogue between two partners asking about markers and answering with an ongoing action.

Solution: Partner 1: "Are there any markers?" Partner 2: "Yes, there are. We're coloring!"

Explanation: Synthesizes both grammar structures into a coherent mini-conversation.

ADVANCED - Example 15

Question: Transform the statement "We are cutting paper" into a question and short answer pair regarding what they are doing.

Solution: Q: "What are you doing?" A: "We're cutting paper."

Explanation: Reverses inquiry and response structure in the first person plural.

15. Explain in English why we use "We're" instead of "I'm" when two students are cutting paper together.

Step-by-Step Solution:

1. "I'm" is singular (one person).

2. When there are two or more people doing an action together, we use the first person plural pronoun "We're".
